# Clinical Data Science Specialization 🩺📊

A comprehensive repository documenting my learning journey, coding labs, and projects through the **Clinical Data Science Specialization** offered by the **University of Colorado** on Coursera[cite: 1].

---

## 📚 Courses & What I Learned

### 1. Introduction to Clinical Data Science
* **Overview:** Introduction to healthcare systems, electronic health records (EHR), and the lifecycle of clinical data.
* **Key Topics:** Clinical workflows, data privacy (HIPAA), and types of health data.

### 2. Clinical Data Models and Data Quality Assessments
* **Overview:** Exploring structured clinical data formats and common data models (CDMs).
* **Key Topics:** Data quality dimensions, data profiling, and handling missing or messy clinical records.

### 3. Identifying Patient Populations
* **Overview:** Methods and criteria for cohort discovery in healthcare databases.
* **Key Topics:** Phenotyping, defining inclusion/exclusion criteria, and analyzing patient cohorts.

### 4. Clinical Natural Language Processing (NLP)
* **Overview:** Extracting valuable insights from unstructured clinical notes and medical text.
* **Key Topics:** Text preprocessing, named entity recognition (NER) in healthcare, and regex/rule-based extraction.

### 5. Predictive Modeling and Transforming Clinical Practice
* **Overview:** Building machine learning models for clinical risk prediction and decision support.
* **Key Topics:** Feature engineering for EHR, model evaluation metrics (Sensitivity, Specificity, AUC), and clinical utility.

### 6. Advanced Clinical Data Science
* **Overview:** Advanced topics, integration of multi-modal data, and deployment considerations in clinical settings.
* **Key Topics:** Advanced analytics, operationalizing models, and translating data science into clinical workflows.
